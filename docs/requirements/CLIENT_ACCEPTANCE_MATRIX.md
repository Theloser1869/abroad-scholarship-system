**CONFLICT detail for REQ-CONTRACT-001/002:**
- **Customer says:** Contract status must move through 11 explicit stages including a dedicated PAYMENT stage and an AMENDED stage, in sequence.
- **Current implementation:** 9-value `ContractStatus` enum with no PAYMENT/AMENDED status values. Payment is modeled as a separate child entity (`Payment`, 1:N off Contract) with its own `PaymentStatus`, not a Contract-level gate. Amendment is modeled as a separate `ContractAmendment` audit-trail entity plus a `Contract.version` counter, not a Contract-level status value.
- **Difference:** The implementation's data model is arguably more correct (a contract can have multiple payments over its life, and "AMENDED" isn't really a resting state — a contract is still ACTIVE while carrying amendment history) — but it does not literally satisfy the customer's stated 11-value sequence, and specifically no code enforces "payment received" before activation.
- **Current DECISION/ASSUMPTION:** Undocumented — no ASM/DEC entry found addressing this specific divergence from the customer's literal contract-status list.
- **Impact:** Low for AMENDED (the audit trail need is met, just not as a status value). Medium for PAYMENT (a contract can currently go SIGNED→ACTIVE with zero payment recorded, which may or may not be intended).
- **Recommendation:** Add a payment-received precondition to the ACTIVE transition; treat AMENDED as satisfied-in-spirit via the amendment/version mechanism and get written client sign-off rather than adding a redundant status value.
- **Customer acceptance required:** YES
